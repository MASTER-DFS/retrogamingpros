The systems

A quarter century of consoles.

From the Atari 2600 in 1977 to the GameCube in 2001, in order of their US release. Pick one to load its file on the TV up top.

    Repairs

    NES and Atari repairs.

    Right now we repair Nintendo Entertainment System and Atari consoles. Tell us what's going on and we'll get back to you about a fix. More systems are on the way.

    Nintendo (NES)

    The front-loading NES is the most-loved console we work on, and its common faults are very fixable.

    • Blinking red light and grey screen
    • 72-pin connector replacement
    • No power or no picture
    • Controller and controller port repair
    • Cartridge cleaning and testing

    Atari

    Woodgrain and all. Atari hardware is simple and tough, which makes it a great candidate for a second life.

    • Won't power on
    • No picture, snowy or rolling picture
    • Sticky or dead console switches
    • Joystick and controller port repair
    • Cartridge slot cleaning
    Community

    Right to repair

    Your console is yours. You should be able to open it, fix it, mod it and keep it running, and we'll help you learn how.

    Preservation

    About

    Made by people who still play these games.

    Retro Gaming Pros is for anyone who still keeps a console hooked up to the TV, or wishes they did. We write about the hardware, the history and the games, and we fix NES and Atari consoles when they stop cooperating.

    We think old games should stay playable, affordable and understood. That means sharing what we learn, supporting the right to repair, and being straight with you about what a fix really takes.
