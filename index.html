The systems

A quarter century of consoles.

From the Atari 2600 in 1977 to the GameCube in 2001, in order of their US release. Pick one to load its file on the TV up top.

    Community

    We play for the community.

    Retro gaming lives on because players, collectors and fixers look out for each other. We stand up for that community every day.

    Right to repair

    Your console is yours. You should be able to open it, fix it, mod it and keep it running, and we'll help you learn how.

    Preservation

    These games and machines are history. Keeping them working, documented and playable matters.

    Free knowledge

    Every guide here is free to read. Sharing what we know about this hardware is the whole point.

    Fair for collectors

    We call out hype pricing and help new players start a collection without overpaying.

    About

    Made by people who still play these games.

    Retro Gaming Pros is for anyone who still keeps a console hooked up to the TV, or wishes they did. We write about the hardware, the history and the games.

    We think old games should stay playable, affordable and understood. That means sharing what we learn and supporting the right to repair.
