How it works

From the bench back to your couch.

  1. Send it in

    Drop it off or mail it with the repair ticket. We email you when it arrives.

  2. Free diagnosis

    We open it up, test every board, and find the real fault.

  3. Approve the quote

    You get a clear price. Nothing is repaired until you say go.

  4. Repair & burn-in

    Fixed, cleaned, and play-tested for at least an hour with real games.

  5. Back to you

    Packed safe, shipped insured, and covered for 90 days.

About the shop

Repaired by people who still play these games.

We started fixing consoles for friends and never stopped. Today we restore hundreds of systems a year, from yellowed SNES shells to N64s that haven't powered on since 2001.

We use quality capacitors, real replacement parts and proper solder work. No glue, no shortcuts. If a console can't be saved, we tell you straight and don't charge for the look.
